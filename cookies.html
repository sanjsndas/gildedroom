    Cookies Policy

    What cookies are, how we use them on Gilded Room Theory, and how you can manage your preferences.

    In short: We use cookies to make the site work, understand how readers use it, and occasionally to remember your preferences. You are in control — you can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies at any time.

    Last updated: 15 January 2026

    This Cookies Policy explains how Gilded Room Theory ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on gildedroomtheory.com (the "Site"). It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal information more broadly.

    1. What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site owners.

    Cookies do not typically contain any information that personally identifies you, but personal information that we store about you may be linked to the information stored in and obtained from cookies. For more information about cookies, including how to see what cookies have been set and how to manage and delete them, visit www.aboutcookies.org.

    2. How We Use Cookies

    We use cookies for the following purposes:

    • Strictly necessary cookies: These are essential for the Site to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. You cannot opt out of these cookies.
    • Performance and analytics cookies: These help us understand how visitors interact with the Site by collecting information anonymously. We use this data to improve the Site and our editorial content.
    • Functionality cookies: These allow the Site to remember choices you make (such as your language or region) and provide enhanced, more personal features.
    • Targeting or advertising cookies: We do not currently use advertising cookies on the Site. Should this change, we will update this policy and provide you with appropriate choices.

    3. Specific Cookies We Use

    Below is a list of the main cookies we use and their purposes. This list may be updated from time to time as our services evolve.

    Cookie name Type Purpose Duration
    grt_session Strictly necessary Preserves your session state across page requests. Session
    grt_consent Strictly necessary Stores your cookie consent preferences. 12 months
    _ga Analytics (Google Analytics) Used to distinguish users and track site usage. 2 years
    _gid Analytics (Google Analytics) Used to distinguish users. 24 hours
    _gat Analytics (Google Analytics) Used to throttle request rate. 1 minute
    grt_prefs Functionality Remembers your display and layout preferences. 6 months

    4. Third-Party Cookies

    Some cookies on the Site are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control the operation of these cookies. The third parties that may set cookies when you use the Site include:

    • Google Analytics: For understanding how readers use the Site. You can learn about Google's privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy, and opt out of Google Analytics at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
    • Email newsletter provider: If you subscribe to our newsletter, our email service provider may use cookies to track engagement (such as whether you opened an email). These are governed by the provider's own privacy and cookie policies.

    5. How to Manage Cookies

    You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by:

    • Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can usually find these in the "Options" or "Preferences" menu of your browser. The following links may help:
      • Google Chrome
      • Mozilla Firefox
      • Safari
      • Microsoft Edge
    • Opt-out tools: For analytics cookies, you can use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on linked above.
    • Do Not Track: Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature. We currently do not respond to Do Not Track signals, but we will continue to monitor developments in this area.

    Please note that if you disable or refuse cookies, some parts of the Site may become inaccessible or not function properly.

    6. Cookies and Personal Data

    Where cookies collect information that could be considered personal data (such as an IP address), we process that data in accordance with our Privacy Policy. Our legal basis for processing cookie data is typically your consent (where required) or our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Site.

    7. Changes to This Cookies Policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. When we make changes, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

    8. Contact Us

    If you have any questions about our use of cookies or this Cookies Policy, please contact us:

    • Email: privacy@gildedroomtheory.com
    • Postal: Gilded Room Theory · 14 Orchard Mews · London · W1T 3QT · United Kingdom

    For more information about how we handle your personal information, please see our Privacy Policy.

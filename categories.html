    How We Organise the Archive

    Gilded Room Theory publishes long-form editorial, not listicles. Each category is curated by our editors to reflect a coherent line of thinking. You will not find SEO filler here — every essay is commissioned, edited, and fact-checked.

    If you are new to the publication, we recommend starting with The Honest Wall: Why Materials Matter More Than Ever or The 80-Centimeter Rule: Circulation in Small Homes. Both are representative of the tone and depth we aim for.

    Suggest a Topic

    We welcome pitches and suggestions from readers, architects, and writers. If there is a subject you believe deserves the Gilded Room Theory treatment, please get in touch. We read every message.
